InterSystems will serve as a launch partner for Google Cloud's Gemini for Healthcare, the company announced today. InterSystems is a creative data technology provider that powers more than one billion health records globally. Under the collaboration, Gemini for Healthcare will connect to the [InterSystems IRIS FHIR MCP](https://financetech-news.com/echelon-payments-strategic-investment-in-create/) server. Consequently, healthcare organizations can give AI assistants and agents governed access to FHIR data.

### **How the Gemini for Healthcare Integration Works**

The integration combines Google Cloud's Gemini capabilities with standards-based data access from InterSystems IRIS FHIR MCP. InterSystems FHIR MCP serves the open Model Context Protocol straight from a FHIR endpoint. As a result, MCP-enabled clients can discover and use FHIR tools. These tools let users search, read, create, update, and manage healthcare data.

Furthermore, the setup links AI access to a recognized interoperability standard. Hospitals and health systems depend on trusted data, so this link carries real weight. In addition, governed access keeps security and oversight at the center of each AI interaction.

InterSystems President Don Woodlock explained the reasoning behind the partnership.

"Healthcare organizations are looking for practical ways to apply AI to their data without compromising security, governance, or interoperability," said Don Woodlock, president of InterSystems. "By connecting Gemini for healthcare with InterSystems IRIS FHIR MCP, we are establishing a trusted bridge between advanced AI and the health information these organizations rely on."

### **Google Cloud Sees Interoperability as the Key to Agentic AI**

Google Cloud shares that focus on safe, connected data. Aashima Gupta, global managing director of Healthcare at Google Cloud, explained why interoperability matters for the next phase of healthcare AI.

"The leap from passive chatbots to agentic workflows depends entirely on interoperability. An AI agent is only as safe and competent as the pipeline feeding it," said Aashima Gupta, global managing director, Healthcare Google Cloud. "Partnering with InterSystems grounds Gemini for healthcare in the bedrock of global healthcare data infrastructure, turning static medical records into active, governed intelligence that works safely at the bedside and beyond."

Overall, the launch partnership signals a push toward secure, standards-based healthcare AI. Healthcare organizations now gain a governed path to link AI agents with trusted health data. Meanwhile, interoperability remains the foundation for every agentic workflow in healthcare. Together, InterSystems and Google Cloud are building that foundation around Gemini for Healthcare.
