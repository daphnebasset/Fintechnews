**ICE Power Futures and Options Set Record 1.7 Billion MWh in Open Interest**
=============================================================================

Intercontinental Exchange, Inc. (ICE), a leading provider of financial market technology and data, has announced that open interest in its North American **ICE power futures and options** has climbed to a record 1.7 billion megawatt hours (MWh). Options account for about one third of that total. In November, options open interest hit a record 573 million MWh, up 13% from a year earlier.

**Trading Volume Also Reaches New Highs**
-----------------------------------------

Trading activity is setting records too. ICE's North American financial power options have traded 2 billion MWh so far this year, already more than the full-year 2025 total. Average daily volume (ADV) has reached a record 9.1 million MWh. Both total volume and ADV are up 25% year over year.

**What Is Driving the Demand**
------------------------------

Brian Lewis, VP of North American Natural Gas and Power at ICE, says market participants now treat extreme weather, data center growth and tighter supply as central concerns. They are turning to ICE futures and options to price those risks. He notes that options are especially useful for hedging exposure that is hard to size or time, which describes much of today's power market. Demand is growing in unpredictable ways, and heat waves, cold snaps and a thin supply cushion can strain the grid with little warning.

**PJM and ERCOT Lead Options Growth**
-------------------------------------

Much of the growth in options has come from contracts tied to PJM Interconnection, which operates the grid across 13 states and the District of Columbia in the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest. ADV for PJM options is up 24% year to date.

ICE's ERCOT options have also performed strongly. These contracts let participants hedge electricity price risk in Texas, and their ADV has risen 82% so far this year.

**ICE Broadens Its Power Derivatives Suite**
--------------------------------------------

ICE offers one of the most comprehensive power derivatives lineups in the U.S., with more than 400 financially settled futures and 60 options contracts. This gives market participants the tools to hedge and manage risk at scale. ICE also operates some of the largest and most liquid energy derivatives markets, covering global power, natural gas, freight, oil and environmental products.

**The Bottom Line**
-------------------

The record open interest and volume point to growing demand for risk management tools in North American power. Weather extremes, data center growth and tight supply continue to reshape the market, and participants are increasingly relying on ICE power futures and options to manage that exposure.
